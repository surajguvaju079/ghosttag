# Audience and content rating — owner action required

Suggested category: Social. Complete the actual IARC questionnaire in Play Console
truthfully; do not invent an Everyone/Teen/Mature rating or equate a product age
threshold with the assigned content rating. Record questionnaire date and results.

Describe real-time user interaction, anonymous room-scoped identities, text UGC,
message reporting, lack of automatic filtering, absence of implemented blocking,
and actual moderation coverage. Text-only chat can still contain sexual language,
threats or hateful content. No images/video are uploaded by this MVP, and no paid
purchases, gambling, public profiles or location-sharing feature is implemented.

Confirm target age groups, neutral screening plan, countries, access restrictions,
reviewer instructions and child-safety duties before rollout. Do not market this
build to children or claim it is safe for minors while these decisions are pending.
Review the current [content rating guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655)
and [target audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159).
