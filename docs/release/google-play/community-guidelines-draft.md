# Community guidelines — DRAFT, publication and operations pending

Proposed rules: be respectful; no harassment, hate, credible threats, sexual
exploitation, nonconsensual content, private-information exposure, fraud, spam,
impersonation or circumvention of safety measures. Child sexual abuse and
exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sexualization
of minors are strictly prohibited in the proposed published standards.

Current tools: report a message from its Report action, choose the matching reason
and confirm. Reporting is private and rate limited. It does not automatically remove
content or guarantee a response. Server moderation actions require authorized
operators. Blocking is not yet implemented. Users can leave an accessible room;
leaving is not a block or deletion of prior messages.

Before release the owner must publish these rules, staff reviews, establish
urgent child-safety/threat escalation, designate contact/coverage, approve sanctions
and appeals, and align evidence handling with law and the retention policy.
Do not claim 24/7 review or a response-time guarantee until coverage exists.
